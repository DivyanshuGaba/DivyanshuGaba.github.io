Connect 4 AI Arena

A game-playing AI built from scratch in Python, with Minimax and alpha-beta pruning and Monte Carlo Tree Search, served through a FastAPI backend with real time WebSocket streaming. Play the AI, watch two AIs compete live, or play another person online with matchmaking, friend invite links, and an Elo rating leaderboard. Deployed on AWS behind a custom HTTPS domain.

  • Minimax at depth 4 achieves 100% win rate against random baseline in 0.111s per game, outperforming MCTS at 500 iterations by 6x speed
  • Real time online multiplayer over WebSockets: Redis backed matchmaking queue, private invite links, reconnect window with forfeit handling, and Elo ratings stored in PostgreSQL
  • Production AWS deployment: EC2 with Docker and Nginx, RDS PostgreSQL, S3 and CloudFront frontend, Secrets Manager for credentials, CloudWatch logs and alarms with SNS email alerts
  • Responsive canvas UI with light and dark themes, keyboard play and reduced motion support; three layer architecture (engine/AI/API), pytest suite, Docker, GitHub Actions CI
